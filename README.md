🌿 POF Tracker
A Player-Owned Farm growth timer and buyer reset tracker for RuneScape 3, built as an Alt1 Toolkit overlay app but also works as standalone in browser.

Option 1 — Direct link - paste this into your Alt1 browser:
alt1://addapp/https://cheese-i.github.io/pof-tracker-v1/appconfig.json

Option 2 — Alt1 browser 
Open Alt1 Toolkit
Click the browser icon in the toolbar
Navigate to: https://cheese-i.github.io/pof-tracker-v1/

From here you can also click the Add App button in the top-right corner of the Alt1 browser

✨ Features

**Currently only for Manor Farm

All 6 pens tracked — Small I & II, Medium I & II, Large I & II
Animals per pen limits: Small 6, Medium 4, Large 3

Manor Farm Animals: Rabbit, Chicken, Chinchompa, Sheep, Spider, Zygomite, Cow, Yak, Dragon

Breed selection for each animal slot, including shiny variants 
Growth stage tracking — Egg → Child → Adolescent → Adult → Elder

Goal-based countdown timers — set a target of Adolescent or Elder
Intermediate stage breakdown — see every stage on the way to your goal
Progress bar for the current growth stage

Buyer reset timers — countdowns to the next buyer for Small (daily), Medium (every 2 days), and Large (every 3 days) pens
Bean sell values displayed per animal per stage, with ★ MAX marker on Adolescent

Notifications — audio beep + visual flash when a goal is reached - sound can be toggled on/off

🔔 Two-note tone for Adolescent, three-note tone for Elder
