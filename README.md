🌿 POF Tracker
A Player-Owned Farm growth timer and buyer reset tracker for RuneScape 3, built as an Alt1 Toolkit overlay app, also works as standalone. 

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
** work in progress **

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Option 1 — Direct link (easiest)
Paste this into your browser while Alt1 is running:
alt1://addapp/https://cheese-i.github.io/pof-tracker-v1/appconfig.json

Option 2 — Alt1 built-in browser (most reliable)

Open Alt1 Toolkit
Click the browser icon in the toolbar
Navigate to: https://cheese-i.github.io/pof-tracker-v1/
Click the Add App button in the top-right corner of the Alt1 browser



---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

✨ Features

All 6 pens tracked — Small I & II, Medium I & II, Large I & II
Up to 6 animals per pen (correct per-pen limits: Small 6, Medium 4, Large 3)
All animals supported — Rabbit, Chicken, Chinchompa, Sheep, Spider, Zygomite, Cow, Yak, Dragon
Breed selection for each animal slot, including shiny variants
Growth stage tracking — Egg → Child → Adolescent → Adult → Elder
Goal-based countdown timers — set a target of Adolescent or Elder and watch the clock count down
Intermediate stage breakdown — see every stage on the way to your goal
Progress bar for the current growth stage
Buyer reset timers — live countdowns to the next buyer for Small (daily), Medium (every 2 days), and Large (every 3 days) pens
Bean sell values displayed per animal per stage, with ★ MAX marker on Adolescent
Notifications — audio beep + visual flash when a goal is reached

🔔 Two-note tone for Adolescent, three-note chord for Elder
Alt1 overlay banner fires directly on your RS client screen


Sound toggle — enable/disable audio alerts, preference saved between sessions
Data persists between sessions via localStorage
